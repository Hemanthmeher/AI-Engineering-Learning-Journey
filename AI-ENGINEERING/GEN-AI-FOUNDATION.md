# Section 2: Generative AI Foundations

Summary and notes for Section 2 covering LLM mechanics, Prompt Engineering, and Generative AI concepts.

- The shift from traditional ML/Deep Learning to Generative AI.

### 2. LLM Architecture & Mechanics

- **Context Windows:** Token limits and handling conversation memory.
- **Hallucinations:** Understanding why LLMs confidently output false information.
- **Temperature:** Controlling randomness (`0.0` for precision, `0.7+` for creativity).

### 3. Prompt Engineering
- **System vs. User Prompts:** Setting guardrails/roles vs. actual instructions.
- **Few-Shot Prompting:** Providing examples within the prompt to improve accuracy.
- **Model Evaluation:** Comparing GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama based on use-cases.

### 4. Modern AI System Architecture
- How AI applications orchestrate prompts, memory, and LLMs.
- The role of frameworks like LangChain in real-world AI architecture.
